# Audio credits

All audio in `Assets/Resources/Audio/` was chosen for Redline from freely licensed sources. Music is **CC BY 4.0** (attribution required, see below); all SFX are **CC0 1.0** (public domain, no attribution required, listed for provenance). Licences were checked on the source pages (incompetech licence page + llms.txt catalog statement; OpenGameArt page "License(s)" field per pack). Music was transcoded MP3 -> OGG Vorbis; SFX transcoded to OGG Vorbis; no other edits.
